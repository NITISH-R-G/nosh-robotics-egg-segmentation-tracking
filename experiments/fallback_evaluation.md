## What a better egg classifier could and could not fix

The pool has 19 candidates in 13 images, 12 egg-like of which 6 clean (`egg`) and 6 bad-boundary (including the uncertain D117-c23). A perfect egg/not-egg classifier would accept all 12: 0 false positives but a clean share of 0.50 against the 0.6 criterion. Boundary quality is a property of the proposal, not of the classifier, so no egg scorer can satisfy that criterion on this pool; only accepting clean eggs and rejecting partial/occluded views would, and that needs a boundary-quality signal.

## Is CLIP justified? (facts checked read-only on 2026-10-29; nothing installed)

- `open-clip-torch` 3.3.0 on PyPI: licence MIT; requires torch>=2.0, torchvision, regex, ftfy, tqdm, huggingface-hub, safetensors, timm>=1.0.17 (several new packages, `timm` the largest).
- `transformers` 5.21.1: Apache 2.0, requires Python >= 3.11, huggingface-hub, tokenizers, safetensors and others.
- `openai/clip-vit-base-patch32` on the Hugging Face hub: single `pytorch_model.bin` of about 577 MB; the hub API returned no licence tag, so the weights' licence is **not verified** here. Weights must be downloaded once (online), then can be used offline. Cost for about 270 crops is small on the existing GPU.
- A text-image similarity is not a calibrated probability; any threshold would have to be chosen leave-one-scene-out like the descriptors.
- Measured justification: the errors of the appearance fallback are (a) boundary quality of occluded and submerged eggs, which a classifier cannot change (see above), (b) three egg-tray cavities in one image (42), egg-shaped depressions that a zero-shot egg prompt may also accept (untested), and (c) one hand-held egg (20) scored low. That is not enough measured benefit to justify the dependencies and an unverified weight licence now.

## Decision

**Retain S1. No fallback is accepted and nothing is frozen.** The appearance fallback fails two of the pre-registered criteria, so it is not adopted automatically. It does show that lightness and interior texture carry signal in the pooled data, but the signal is weakest in the scenes where S1 needs help (steel pot with water, egg tray, white on white) and thresholds chosen out-of-scene give low precision on the hard subset.

## Limitations

- 14 egg-like candidates in the hard subset (13 flagged images): tiny sample; intervals are wide and the scene bootstrap has only 15 scenes.
- Descriptors and the fallback pool gates were designed after seeing these same dev failures: results are exploratory and optimistic.
- Verdicts, counts and scene categories are AI-assisted; one candidate (D117-c23) is uncertain and counted as bad-boundary.
- The reviewed pool is not a random sample of candidates: it holds only candidates some configuration selected.
- The evaluation split has not been touched; nothing here says how any method behaves on it.
